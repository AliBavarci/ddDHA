03 · FAQ

Frequently Asked.

Why are prices in Ethereum (ETH) instead of USD? +

Ethereum is the native currency of the blockchain we work on (smart contracts, NFTs, decentralized infrastructure). Using ETH allows us to collaborate in a shared financial language, without traditional banking intermediaries, and with lower fees.

Is it possible to work with a limited budget? +

Yes. We offer lighter packages for artists, students, and small projects. We also offer milestone payments and revenue-sharing collaborations for specific projects.

Do I get full ownership of the code and files? +

Yes. In all packages, you receive full ownership of all code, files, and digital assets, along with complete documentation.

Do you offer refunds? +

For custom art projects (digital paintings, NFTs), due to their personalized nature, refunds are not available. For consulting and technical projects, if you are not satisfied after the first milestone, the full amount is refunded.
