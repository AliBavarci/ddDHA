— About the Studio —

A studio in the interstice.

We are an experimental workshop operating at the boundary between technology and artistic expression.

01 · Philosophy

Technology is material.

Digital Hana Arts began as a thought experiment: If we treat computation like paint and canvas, like brick and mortar, what can we build?

The answer led us to the intersection of four domains — Art, Data, AI, and Blockchain — and subsequently to intelligent market forecasting.

We are neither solely an "art studio" nor a "tech company." We stand exactly where tool and artifact become one.

02 · Core Values

Four Principles.

01

Discovery

Every project is a new question. We ask before we assume we know the answer.

02

Transparency

Open code, open data, complete documentation — our belief in shared knowledge.

03

Beauty

Algorithms can be beautiful. Beauty is a core metric of our work.

04

Human-Centric

Technology is a tool in service of humans — not the reverse. Hana reminds us of this covenant.

03 · Origin of the Name

Meet Hana.

"Hana" is the name of the kitten at the heart of our studio. In fact, Hana existed before the first line of code or algorithm.

She is a reminder that amidst all the computation, data, and networks, something intentionally human and organic must remain: curiosity, individuality, and character.

That is why Hana's images and NFTs are an inseparable part of the DHA visual identity.
